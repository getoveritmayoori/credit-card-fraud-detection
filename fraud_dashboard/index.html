Domain Alignment & Insights (Task 4.2 & 4.3)

Why Feature Importances Align with Banking Risk Domain:

  • amount_vs_avg_ratio & amount_to_limit_ratio: Fraudulent transactions rarely follow historical cardholder spending limits. Spikes in amount relative to 30-day baseline strongly signal card takeover.
  • is_night_transaction & CNP mode: Unauthorized transactions peak between 10 PM and 4 AM when cardholders are asleep and card-not-present e-commerce channels bypass physical PIN security.
  • velocity_surge_ratio: Fraudsters test stolen card details in rapid succession before cardholders realize and request card blockage.
